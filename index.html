Long-press the photo thumbnail → this panel. Tap VIDEO / PHOTO to switch modes. The record button really records (the overlay included) – tap the thumbnail to play it back or share it.

Two-finger tap on the picture → start the fake card (timed mode) / arm-disarm (tracking mode). Pressing Record can also start it.

Three-finger tap on the picture → next card.

Phone face up, front camera, still. Let it see the empty scene for a second before the card comes in (it learns the background). The card can be darker or lighter than the background – it just has to be different, white-ish and flat to the lens.

"Card size when cut off" is learnt automatically each time the whole card is seen; it's used to place the card while it's still sliding in.
